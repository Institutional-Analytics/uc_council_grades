LIU Fall 2025-2026 Academic Performance Dashboard

University Council Presentation | Comprehensive Analysis of Student Academic Performance

Executive Summary

  • Total Students: 30,524 students enrolled in Fall 2025-2026
  • Total Course Grades: 139,177 grades recorded across all courses
  • Good Academic Standing: 66.0% of students (20,145) have GPA ≥ 2.0
  • Academic Probation: 34.0% of students (10,379) have GPA < 2.0
  • Course Pass Rate: 84.2% of course grades resulted in passing
  • Average Student GPA: 2.32 across all students
  • SoEN: Highest probation rate at 40.2%
  • Graduate Students: 79.6% in good standing vs 64.8% for undergraduates
Key Concern: Over one-third (34%) of students are under academic probation. This represents 10,379 students who may need additional academic support. SoEN has the highest probation rate at 40.2%.
