LIU Fall 2025-2026 Academic Performance Dashboard

University Council Presentation | Comprehensive Analysis of Student Academic Performance

Executive Summary

  • Total Students: 29,338 students enrolled in Fall 2025-2026
  • Total Course Grades: 139,177 grades recorded across all courses
  • Good Academic Standing: 77.6% of students (22,772) have cumulative GPA ≥ 2.0
  • Academic Probation: 22.4% of students (6,566) have cumulative GPA < 2.0
  • Course Pass Rate: 84.2% of course grades resulted in passing
  • Average Cumulative GPA: 2.59 across all students
  • SoEN: Highest cumulative probation rate at 29.8%
  • Graduate Students: 85.3% in good standing vs 77.0% for undergraduates (cumulative GPA)
Note: 1,186 students with zero attempted credits (both current and cumulative) were excluded from all metrics. These students are enrolled but have never attempted any coursework.
